# Facts: Short 02 "Automatic transmission, cut in half"

Every claim in the narration and on screen, with its source. Checked 2026-11-09.

| Claim (as said / shown) | Basis | Source |
|---|---|---|
| "Fewer than two in a hundred new cars in America have a clutch pedal." On screen: "Under 2% of new US cars have a clutch pedal." | EPA data: manual transmissions under 1% of US new-vehicle production since model year 2021. WardsAuto puts manual sales at 1.7% in 2023. The wording "fewer than two in a hundred" holds under both sources. | EPA Automotive Trends Report (as reported by InsideHook, The Autopian); WardsAuto via AutoGuide |
| "From a stop, the engine doesn't touch them." It spins a fan (pump/impeller) in oil, and the oil spins a second fan (turbine) | A torque converter is a fluid coupling. The impeller is driven by the engine, and the turbine is connected to the transmission input. Pulling away, the lock-up clutch is open, so power goes only through the fluid. At cruise, the lock-up clutch can join the pump and turbine; that is why the line says "from a stop", not "never". | Wikipedia "Torque converter"; aa1car.com torque converter guide |
| "That's why you can stop without stalling." | A fluid coupling lets the engine keep running while the vehicle is stationary. | Same as above |
| Simplification, not said: the stator and lock-up clutch. Labels: "pump · driven by engine" (it sits on the transmission side of the converter) and "turbine · drives the gears" | The stator multiplies torque at low speed; a lock-up clutch joins the pump and turbine at cruise. The film shows the stator but does not name it. | Same as above |
| Hold the ring: the output crawls (low). On screen: 3.4 : 1 | Simple planetary set with the sun in, the ring held and the carrier out: ratio = 1 + R/S = 1 + 68/28 = 3.43 (the film's set: sun 28 teeth, ring 68, planets 20). | Planetary gear kinematics (Willis equation) |
| Lock it all together: 1 : 1 | Any two members locked together make the whole set turn as one. | Same |
| Hold the carrier: it turns backwards (reverse) | Carrier held: ring = −S/R × sun = −28/68 (opposite direction, 2.43 : 1 reduction). | Same |
| Clutches do the holding, squeezed by oil, switched by a computer | Shift elements are hydraulically applied clutches. The control unit switches solenoids in the valve body; the 10R80's valve body has eight solenoids. | Rick's Free Auto Repair Advice (10R80); Gears Magazine |
| "Ford and GM's ten-speed stacks four of these sets." On screen: "10 speeds · 4 gear sets · 6 clutches" | The 10R80 was co-developed by Ford and GM: ten forward ratios from four simple planetary gearsets and six clutches. | Gears Magazine; The Truth About Cars teardown; The Ranger Station |

## Picture notes (honest about the model)
- The transmission in the film is a **generic** layout drawn in code: torque converter, four simple planetary sets, clutch packs, valve body. It is **not** a 10R80 to scale. The 10R80 is mentioned only for its count of sets and clutches.
- Speeds are slowed down for clarity. The torque-converter sequence turns at about 60 rpm on screen.
- In each film set, the gears mesh correctly: the planets and ring are posed from the sun and carrier angles by tooth mesh.
