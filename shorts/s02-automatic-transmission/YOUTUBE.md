## Description
An automatic transmission, cut in half: a fan in oil, one set of planetary gears, and clutches that choose which part holds still.

- Torque converter: the engine spins a pump in transmission fluid, and the fluid spins a turbine. That's why the car can stop without the engine stalling. (At cruising speed, a lock-up clutch can join the two for efficiency.)
- Planetary gears (sun 28 teeth, ring 68, planets 20 in this model): hold the ring and the output turns 3.4 times slower (1 + 68/28). Lock it together and you get 1:1. Hold the carrier and the output turns backwards.
- Clutches, applied by oil pressure and switched by the transmission computer, decide which part is held.
- Ford and GM's 10-speed (10R80) uses four planetary gear sets and six clutches.

The transmission shown is a generic layout drawn in code, not a specific model to scale. Motion is slowed for clarity.
