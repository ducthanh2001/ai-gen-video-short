## Narration (one line = one beat; no dashes anywhere)
1. He promised the king a box of gears that could predict eclipses, and the whole court laughed.
2. You see, picture a craftsman in a world where the sky belongs to the gods.
3. The king gave him one chance: be right, or lose your head.
4. The craftsman said nothing. He kept cutting gears.
5. Weeks later, he turned the crank. The dials said: forty days.
6. The court laughed harder. Nobody tells the Sun when to vanish.
7. Forty days later, at noon, the sky went dark.
8. Nobody laughed.
9. We don't know his name.
10. But in 1901, sponge divers pulled a corroded lump of bronze from a Roman shipwreck.
11. X-rays looked inside: thirty bronze gears. One matched the 19-year cycle of the Moon. Another, the 223-month cycle of eclipses.
12. An eclipse calculator, over two thousand years old.
13. The sea kept his machine. History lost the man.
