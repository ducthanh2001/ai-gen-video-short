## Description
One month of rent, measured in hours of minimum-wage work.

1970: median rent $108 a month, federal minimum wage $1.60 an hour = 67.5 hours.
Today: median rent $1,487 a month, federal minimum wage $7.25 an hour (unchanged since July 24, 2009) = 205 hours.
A full-time month is about 173 hours (40 h x 52 weeks / 12).

Sources:
- U.S. Census Bureau, Historical Census of Housing Tables: Median Gross Rents (1970, unadjusted)
- U.S. Census Bureau, American Community Survey 2024 1-year, table B25064 (median gross rent)
- U.S. Department of Labor / FRED series FEDMINNFRWG (federal minimum wage)

Notes: gross rent = contract rent + utilities; national medians; federal minimum wage (many states set a higher minimum today). In 1970 the $1.60 rate applied to workers covered before the 1966 amendments.
