# FACTS: Short 01 "Rent, 1970 vs today"

Every number on screen or in the narration, with its source. Checked 2026-11-09.

| # | Claim (as the film says it) | Source | Status | Line |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | U.S. median gross rent, 1970: $108 a month | U.S. Census Bureau, Historical Census of Housing Tables, Median Gross Rents (unadjusted): https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/decennial/tables/time-series/coh-grossrents/grossrents-unadj.txt | confirmed (via search excerpt of the Census file; direct download blocked by Cloudflare) | a1 |

| 3 | U.S. median gross rent, 2024: $1,487 a month | U.S. Census Bureau, ACS 2024 1-year, table B25064 (via gigafact.org fact brief and statranker.org, both citing B25064); data.census.gov blocked for automated access | confirmed by two independent citations of the same table; re-check on data.census.gov before publishing | b1 |
| 4 | Federal minimum wage today: $7.25, unchanged since July 24, 2009 | U.S. DOL; multiple 2026 reports (e.g. Spectrum News, 2026-01-02) | confirmed | b2 |
| 5 | 1970: $108 / $1.60 = 67.5 hours | arithmetic | confirmed | a3 |
| 6 | Today: $1,487 / $7.25 = 205.1 hours ("two hundred and five") | arithmetic | confirmed | b3 |
