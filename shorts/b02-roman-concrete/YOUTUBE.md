## Description
A dramatised story. The builder and the sea wall are imagined; the science is real.

Roman builders mixed quicklime straight into their concrete. It left small white lumps of lime in the stone, which people long took for sloppy work. In 2023, researchers from MIT and Harvard showed why they matter: when a crack reaches a lump, water dissolves the lime and it hardens again as calcium carbonate, sealing the crack. Their hot-mixed test samples sealed within two weeks. The same mix without quicklime never healed.
